# Sign-up "A5 — a living scene" — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** Rebuild the website's sign-up screen and the one-time "choose your role" screen in Alex's approved A5 design. The left side becomes one living, role-specific scene: sky, sun or moon, scene, pins, trail, a floating phone with the AR result, an example trail card and a "live moment" chip. The right side holds the form. Phones get the A5 phone layout. The age question is hidden for the competition.

**Architecture:**
- The visuals are server-rendered Astro components: `RoleScene.astro` (5 scenes, day/night colours), `PhoneAR.astro` (5 phone screens) and `RoleStage.astro` (the whole left panel).
- All copy and data per role lives in `web/src/lib/role.ts` (`stageFor(role, work, lang)`).
- The existing client picker script repaints the stage when the choice changes. All 6 states are rendered once and toggled with `hidden`, or swapped via `data-` attributes. No rendering on the client side.

**Tech Stack:** Astro 7 static site, CSS keyframes, inline SVG, Playwright lab tests.

## Global Constraints

- **Label swap (Alex, 10 Nov).** Each job is presented by its field first, with the role word as the subtext.

  | Job | Header BG | Subtext BG | Header EN | Subtext EN |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | teacher | Образование | Учител | Education | Teacher |
  | guide | Туризъм | Екскурзовод | Tourism | Tour guide |
  | broker | Недвижими имоти | Брокер | Real estate | Estate broker |
  | other | Друго | Всички възможности | Other | Every option |

  - The stage chip reads `<header> · създател` (e.g. "Образование · създател"), or "Зрител" for viewers.
  - Apply the swap everywhere a job is shown or chosen: sign-up, choose-role, Account settings, the "Стани създател" card and dialog, and the header badge aria-label. The DB values don't change.
- **Age question hidden for the competition.**
  - A config flag `ASK_AGE = false` in one place, e.g. `web/src/lib/config.ts` or the existing site config.
  - When it's false: no age fieldset, no parent fields, and sign-up sends `age_band: '14+'` exactly as a 14+ choice does today.
  - When it's true: today's behaviour comes back unchanged.
  - Keep all parent/consent code paths intact.
- **Theme.** Light uses the A5 day skies. Dark follows the site's existing dark theme switch and uses the A5 night skies, moon and stars.
  - Day skies: teacher `#d3ecd6`, guide `#f9e2bd`, broker `#cfe5f8`, other `#e3e8f0`, viewer `#d0ece2`, none `#ecedf0`.
  - Night skies: teacher `#0f2a22`, guide `#2b2114`, broker `#0d2238`, other `#1b2130`, viewer `#0f2a24`, none `#17191c`.
- **Animations** exactly as in A5: drift, bob, drop, draw, pop, twinkle. They play for everyone, matching the site's existing decision on motion (see the `town.ts` header comment).
- **Honesty.** Trail cards stay labelled "Примерен маршрут". Every claim must match a real feature.
- **Accessibility.** All decoration is `aria-hidden`. The stage headline is an `aria-live="polite"` region. The form keeps real radios and labels, 44 px targets and visible focus.
- **No page-level sideways scroll at 390 px.**

## Review Focus

1. Switching role or job repaints the whole stage: sky, scene, phone, card, moment, chip, headline and signs. Nothing stale remains from the previous role.
2. Light/dark switch while the page is open: the stage follows.
3. Sign-up still works end to end:
   - the role and work metadata;
   - the guest upgrade;
   - validation errors next to the choices;
   - the hidden age sends 14+.
4. Choose-role keeps all R2 behaviour: pre-fill from `work`, the safe `next`, saving role and work.
5. Performance: no layout shift on load; the animations are CSS only.
