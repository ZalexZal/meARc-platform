# Content moderation (EU Digital Services Act)

meARc stores content its users publish (trails, stops, media, questions,
team names) and shows it to others, so it is a hosting service and, for the
public library, an online platform. As a micro enterprise it is exempt from
most platform duties (art. 19), but not from these:

| Article | Duty | How meARc meets it |
|---|---|---|
| 11-12 | Points of contact for authorities and users | zalex8623@gmail.com, named in the terms and privacy policy |
| 14 | Terms explain moderation | Terms: "What may and may not be published", "Reporting, blocking and moderation" |
| 16 | Notice and action | Report on every stop, trail and team name in the database; the app's Report buttons are hidden at present; on the website, Report on team names on the board; email |
| 17 | Statement of reasons | Every removal leaves a notice on the creator's My trails: what, when, reasons reported, reviewer's note, ground, that a person decided, how to contest |
| 18 | Report suspected crimes threatening life or safety | See below |

## Handling a report

1. Open the inbox (website → Review) at least every few days while the app is
   public; the same day for anything about a child's safety.
2. Look at the reported item. Decide against the terms, not taste.
3. **Remove** (trail, stop, or disqualify a team for its name) with a short
   note saying which rule was broken - the note reaches the creator - or
   **dismiss** with a note for the record.
4. A creator who contests by email gets a fresh look by the operator, and an
   answer in writing.
5. Repeated or serious breaches: delete the account (Supabase dashboard →
   Authentication → Users) and keep a one-line record of why.

## Serious cases

- Child sexual abuse material: remove at once, do not download or forward
  it, report it to the police (112, or the cybercrime unit of GDBOP) and to
  Bulgaria's Safer Internet hotline, and keep the report reference.
- A threat to someone's life or safety: tell the police (112) straight away
  (DSA art. 18).
- Orders from authorities (art. 9-10): answer without undue delay, and tell
  the affected user unless the order forbids it.
